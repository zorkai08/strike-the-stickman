✏️ HOW TO PLAY

  1. Sketch an item in the Sketch Lab. The shape recognizer will identify it and give you a full-color object.
  2. Press Guess My Drawing. The built-in shape recognizer compares your sketch to known doodles.
  3. The detector picks one best guess. If it is wrong, change your drawing and press Guess again.
  4. Tap empty space in the arena to spawn the colored version of your item. It falls with gravity and bounces off the floor.
  5. Hold and drag a spawned item to move it into the stickman. Ranged and magical items fire automatically while held. Shotguns spread pellets, and flamethrowers create a stream of cartoon fire that turns blue after a continuous 6-second hold and turns the character into a silly ash pile on contact. Grenade launchers fire bouncing cartoon grenades that pop after 3 seconds. Release to stop firing.
  6. Draw a landmine and drop it into the arena. Touching the stickman triggers a cartoon explosion. A Nuke activates when it hits the floor, with an enormous comic-book blast. Explosions break separated pieces into colorful toy fragments.
  7. Hold and drag the stickman (while not holding an item) by an arm, leg, head, or torso. The body hangs naturally from the spot you grab; fling him into the ceiling, floor, or side walls. Repeated powerful impacts can pop off limbs, and strong head-first collisions can eventually pop off the head too.
  8. Hold the Frying Pan for sizzling audio, and listen for the rumbling thunderclap when Lightning or the Spellbook summons a strike. The Trident and Grim Reaper Scythe have updated drawings. Select Goku Boss in Customize → Skins to fight all seven forms, each with a brand-new health bar. Goku sometimes charges a Kamehameha: dodge the marked blue lane with your held weapon or hit Goku during the charge to interrupt. A hit knocks the weapon loose, but you can pick it up again shortly afterward.
  9. Kunai throws one blade per tap, while Throwing Knives launches three. The harpoon has a barbed head and a rope.
  10. Hold the Giant Pencil and scrub back and forth over the stickman to gradually erase him. The Flail and Whip have simulated hanging chain and rope physics.
  11. The top Deaths counter records each stickman's defeat once. Use Reset Stickman to start a new round.
  12. Arsenal: Every item starts locked. Hand-draw an item, press Guess, and tap the arena to summon it and unlock it. If the guess is wrong, redraw it and try again; there are no alternate-choice buttons. Tap an unlocked Arsenal item to equip it, then tap the arena to summon it. Unlocks save automatically. Surprise Me selects only unlocked items.

Items remain in the arena until you clear them. Reset Stickman restores the character, but doesn't erase your spawned drawings. The recognizer works locally without uploading sketches. Use Customize to change names, skins and maps.
